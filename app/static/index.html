How these are calculated

Large prizes are typically withheld at 24% federally; your final rate depends on total income. The banner’s 37% is the top federal marginal rate for a conservative take-home estimate. California does not tax CA Lottery prizes; it usually does tax interest (Treasury interest is often CA-exempt). Interest uses ~4% APY then ~50.3% combined tax (37% federal + 13.3% CA); high earners may also owe ~3.8% NIIT.
