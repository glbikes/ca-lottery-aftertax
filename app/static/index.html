How these are calculated

After-tax cash is the cash option minus 37% federal tax, the top marginal rate, for a conservative take-home estimate. Large prizes are usually withheld at 24% federally; your final rate depends on total income. California does not tax CA Lottery prizes.

The interest line assumes the after-tax cash earns about 4% APY, with that interest taxed at about 50.3% (37% federal plus 13.3% CA). Treasury interest is often CA-exempt, and high earners may also owe about 3.8% NIIT.
